# AI Skillz

A repository of agent skills for planning, evaluating, and operating AI-assisted work.

## Language

**Skill**:
A directory containing a `SKILL.md` file, optional `agents/openai.yaml`, and optional bundled resources.

**Promoted skill**:
A skill in `skills/engineering/` or `skills/productivity/` that is considered ready to advertise from the top-level README.

**Draft skill**:
A skill in `skills/in-progress/`. Draft skills may be incomplete, experimental, or placeholders for future workflows.

**Evaluation plan**:
A living artifact that turns an AI feature or workflow goal into claims, metrics, datasets, scorers, tasks, risks, and progress tracking.
